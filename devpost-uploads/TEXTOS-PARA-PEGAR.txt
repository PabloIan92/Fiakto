========================================
CAMPO 1: "How do you measure impact?"
========================================

Impact will be measured through the product's own request lifecycle rather than a separate analytics layer: number of requests successfully matched and completed, the payout amount released to the professional, and a 1-5 star customer review collected when a request is approved and closed. Comparing Gemini's structured trade/urgency classification against the customer's original free-text description also measures how much manual back-and-forth Gemini removes versus an unstructured referral. No live usage data exists yet, since the product has only been exercised by the founder during testing, not by real customers.

No expenses were incurred during the Hackathon period. Development used a personal computer and internet connection already owned before the Hackathon, plus free or trial tiers of Firebase and Google Cloud services, so there was no new cash outlay attributable to this submission.
