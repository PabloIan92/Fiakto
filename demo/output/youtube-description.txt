Fiakto — a home-repair marketplace built on Gemini, live in production.

A customer describes a problem with a photo and a sentence. Gemini classifies it in seconds (trade, risk, price range, confidence) — no templates, no fallback. Fiakto matches verified professionals automatically, runs private competing quotes with an automatic 8% commission, keeps customer and professional talking inside its own chat (redacting phone numbers/emails before they're even saved), and closes the loop with a photo, an approval, and a real rating.

Built for the Build with Gemini XPRIZE hackathon.
