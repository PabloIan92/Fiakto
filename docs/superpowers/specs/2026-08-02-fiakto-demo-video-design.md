# Fiakto Devpost Demo Video Design

## Goal

Produce an English-language MP4 suitable for YouTube and the Devpost submission. The video should explain Fiakto clearly and attractively without presenting planned functionality as already implemented.

## Format

- 16:9, 1920x1080, H.264 MP4 with AAC audio and web-optimized fast start.
- Target duration: 75–90 seconds.
- English voice-over with burned-in English subtitles.
- Quiet instrumental background music, kept below the narration.
- Fiakto visual identity, ending with “Todo tiene solución.”

## Story Structure

1. **Problem:** Hiring a trustworthy local professional is stressful and opaque.
2. **Input:** A customer shows a home problem with a photo and short description.
3. **Gemini:** Multimodal analysis structures the request, asks follow-up questions, and flags safety risks.
4. **Trusted workflow:** Verified professionals receive matched opportunities and submit private quotes.
5. **Safety and accountability:** Addresses remain protected; Gemini assists but never moves money; consequential actions remain auditable and human-controlled.
6. **Close:** Introduce the Argentina pilot and finish on the Fiakto promise.

## Visual Treatment

Use the real Fiakto landing page where available. Represent the incomplete request, triage, matching, and quote stages with polished animated concept cards labeled “Prototype flow” or “Planned workflow.” Do not simulate clicks or claim that an unfinished workflow is live. Use restrained motion, large mobile-readable typography, high contrast, and concise screen copy.

## Narration

The voice-over should be confident and plain-spoken, avoiding unsupported metrics and absolute claims. Gemini is described as assisting triage and organization, not diagnosing with certainty. The payment flow is described as planned and regulated, not as escrow already implemented.

## Deliverables and Validation

- Final MP4 ready for YouTube upload.
- Thumbnail or representative frame.
- English narration transcript.
- Validate resolution, codecs, duration, audio presence, and successful playback with FFprobe/FFmpeg.
- Review representative frames to confirm legibility, visual continuity, and the visible prototype disclosure.
